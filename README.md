<h1>osTicket - Post-Install Configuration</h1>
This project demonstrates the post installation process for osTicket. This process
ensures that the system is ready to be used by Staff and end users.<br />



<h2>Environments and Technologies Used</h2>

- Microsoft Azure (Virtual Machines/Compute)
- Remote Desktop
- Internet Information Services (IIS)

<h2>Operating Systems Used </h2>

- Windows 10</b> (21H2)

<h2>Post-Install Configuration Objectives</h2>

- Step 1: Role Configuration
- Step 2: Department Configuration
- Step 3: Team Configuration
- Step 4: Agent Configuration
- Step 5: SLA Configuration

<p>Step 1: Role Configuration: The purpose of this step is to define and control what agents are allowed to see and do within the system. This is essential for managing responsobilities and permissions within your support team. 
<br />

<p><img width="1440" alt="Departmantconfigp1" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d08d5d9f-fc7c-4aa1-9bc9-3481bdecedf4" />

<p> Step 2: Department Configuration: Departments represent different functioning areas of your organization including customer service, sales,and IT Support. Creating departments within osTicket ensures that proper tickets reach the correct team or group of agents. These teams or agents all operate differently so they will have their own set of rules including access controls and workflows. 
<br />

<p>
<img width="1440" alt="Teamconfig" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/659482b9-292a-4354-b4bd-77aa1449b520" />
</p>
<p>
Step 3: Team Configuration: Creating teams within osTicket is necessary when you have agents across multiple departments working together on tickets. These teams allow agents to share responsibilities when they are working on tickets that require different levels of expertise. For example their may be a situation where your IT Support team has to work with the online banking team to solve an issue with the mobile app. 
<br />

Step 4: Agent configuration: Agents are the users with in osTicket who are staff members of your organization responsible for handling support tickets. Agents can be assigned to different teams and departments and given specific roles that align to their duties. 
<img width="1440" alt="SLAconfig" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/33dbd458-0871-4c05-8f0b-1b1ba05a7e66" />
Step 5: SLA Configuration: SLA aggrements within osTicket are put into place to define and enforce response and resolution time expectations for support tickets. Their are different levels of severity that support tickets should be assigned to. Tickets with higher severity are usually assigned to an SLA that requires a quicker response time while lower Severity tickets have a larger grace period when it comes to response and resolution.
